\subsection{What a dissociation would mean}

If morphology and meaning recover at different rates, the interesting consequence
is not about cellular automata. It is that in a system where computation is
genuinely distributed, structural repair and functional repair are separable
processes, and a repair mechanism optimised for the first supplies no guarantee
about the second. The organism regrows to specification and speaks a language it
can no longer be understood in. Nothing in its morphological loss ever referred
to the convention, so nothing pulled the convention back.

The relabeling decomposition sharpens this. If the regenerated sender's code is a
\emph{permutation} of the original, then the information survived and only the
alignment was lost --- which is a statement about the arbitrariness of a
convention, not about damage destroying knowledge. Reporting the split rather
than a single agreement number is what makes those two outcomes distinguishable,
and we regard the split as the more durable contribution of the two.

\subsection{Limitations}

\paragraph{Power.} With 8 seeds this study resolves only large effects
($d \gtrsim 1.2$). We state this rather than letting a reader infer that
non-significance means absence. Formal testing is not the norm in this subfield;
we would rather do it and be candid about what it can and cannot detect.

\paragraph{One body, one game.} Every result is on a single target morphology
(a disk), $N=V=8$, and message length 1. The disk was chosen so geodesic distance
is analytic and damage location parameterises cleanly, but rotational symmetry is
a real risk we did not fully control for, and a single morphology cannot separate
properties of this substrate from properties of this shape. Message length 1 also
means compositionality is out of reach: at $L=1$ topographic similarity
degenerates, which is why we do not report it.

\paragraph{The readout could be doing the work.} Mean pooling architecturally
imposes uniform participation on the vote channels, so ``communication is
distributed'' risks being a tautology of the readout rather than a finding about
the organism. Proposition~\ref{prop:deletion} and the frozen-state control
address whether \emph{damage effects} are artifacts, but they do not by
themselves establish that the distributedness is earned. The honest controls are
within-architecture --- comparing the vote readout against the morphology readout
under matched ablation, and normalising against an untrained organism --- and a
learned-readout arm is required before any claim that the parameter-free readout
is responsible for robustness. \gated{these arms}

\paragraph{Damage is not surgery.} Our damage operators zero all channels in a
disk. Real injury is not a disk, and the choice of operator plausibly interacts
with what recovers. We parameterise severity by fraction of currently-alive cells
rather than by radius, which at least prevents severity from being confounded
with body size and regeneration state, but the shape of the wound remains a
designed convenience.

\subsection{Reproducibility}

Every number in this paper traces to a committed artifact --- gate reports,
training logs, and the raw probe field --- and figures regenerate from those logs
rather than from a notebook. We hold ourselves to one rule that the propagation
analysis in Section~\ref{sec:results-probe} initially violated: \textbf{a result
without committed code that regenerates it is not a result}. The decoder analysis
excluded there was excluded for exactly that reason, and its absence from this
paper is deliberate.

Determinism is asserted same-device only. CPU and GPU backends produce different
streams from the same seed, so the device is part of run identity and is recorded
with every run.

\subsection{Future work}

The immediate extensions are the ones this design deliberately cut for scope:
damaging the receiver instead of the sender, which is the mirror experiment and
tests whether the asymmetry is real; activation patching from the healthy twin
into the regenerating organism, which would localise \emph{where} the semantic
information was lost rather than only establishing that it was; and a
compositional extension with 16 referents and message length 2, which is the
smallest setting in which compositionality measures become meaningful. Movement
and reinforcement learning were cut at the outset in favour of a fully
differentiable game, and reintroducing them is the natural path toward organisms
that must communicate to do something rather than to name something.
