\section{Measuring semantic recovery}
\label{sec:protocol}

The neural-cellular-automata literature measures recovery as pixel distance to a
target, or as a downstream task score. The emergent-communication literature has
never needed a notion of recovery at all, because its agents cannot be
structurally damaged. Neither supplies what this question requires. This section
is the second contribution of the paper: a set of definitions under which
``the protocol came back'' is a number rather than an impression.

\paragraph{Notation.} Damage occurs at $t=0$; $\dmg$ and $\pdmg$ denote the state
immediately before and after, and $\Delta$ counts steps since. Every damaged
episode has a \textbf{matched healthy twin} --- identical seed, referent, and RNG
stream, no damage. Recovery is measured against the twin as well as against the
pre-damage state, because a healthy organism also drifts over 256 steps and that
drift must not be counted as damage.

\subsection{Four recoveries, deliberately distinct}

\begin{description}
\item[Morphological.]
  $\MR_t = (\IoU_t - \IoU_\pdmg)/(\IoU_\dmg - \IoU_\pdmg)$. Recovered when
  $\IoU_t \geq 0.95\,\IoU_\dmg$, RMSE within $1.1\times$ its pre-damage value,
  and area ratio in $[0.9, 1.1]$ --- all three \emph{sustained for 20
  consecutive steps}. The sustain requirement eliminates transient touch-and-go
  crossings, which otherwise corrupt every recovery-time statistic.

\item[Behavioural.]
  $\BR_t = (\mathrm{Acc}_t - 1/N)/(\mathrm{Acc}_\dmg - 1/N)$. Chance correction
  is mandatory: reporting that accuracy ``recovered to 50\%'' is meaningless when
  chance is 12.5\%. Recovery is declared by a one-sided \emph{non-inferiority}
  test at margin $\delta = 0.05$, not by failure to reject a null --- failing to
  reject is not evidence of recovery.

\item[Computational.] Behaviour can be right for the wrong reasons, so we also
  ask whether the same cells went back to the same job: centered-logit recovery
  $\geq 0.9$, representational similarity $\geq 0.90$ over hidden channels only,
  and per-cell field correlation $\geq 0.8$. Logits are centered first because
  softmax is shift-invariant and an uncentered offset would read as semantic
  change.

\item[Semantic.]
  $\SIA_t = \frac{1}{N}\sum_r \indicator[\hat\sigma_t(r) = \hat\sigma_\dmg(r)]$,
  macro-averaged over referents so that referent frequency cannot distort it.
\end{description}

These form a \emph{lattice, not a total order}: computational recovery implies
semantic implies behavioural, but not conversely. A permuted code is structurally
intact and behaviourally dead.

\subsection{The split that carries the paper}

Semantic recovery divides into two questions that are usually conflated:
%
\begin{align}
  \mathrm{struct}_t  &= \MI_t(M;R) \,/\, \MI_\dmg(M;R), \\
  \mathrm{align}_t   &= \SIA_t, \\
  \mathrm{relabel}_t &= \SIA^{\mathrm{perm}}_t - \SIA_t .
\end{align}
%
The first asks \emph{does it still speak a language?} and is invariant to any
relabeling of the vocabulary. The second asks \emph{does it speak the same
language?} and is not. The third measures how much of any loss in the second is
pure relabeling rather than lost structure.
%
$\SIA^{\mathrm{perm}}$ maximises agreement over all $V! = 40{,}320$ vocabulary
permutations. We compute it with the Hungarian algorithm on the $V\times V$
pre/post confusion matrix, which is an \emph{exact} maximum in $O(V^3)$ --- this
is a linear assignment problem, not a greedy match, and we state so explicitly
because a reader is right to wonder.

The dissociation hypothesis is then the event
%
\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:h3}
  \MR_t \geq 0.95 \;\wedge\; \mathrm{struct}_t \geq 0.90 \;\wedge\;
  \mathrm{align}_t < 0.60 ,
\end{equation}
%
that is: body perfect, still linguistic, no longer mutually intelligible with its
unchanged partner.

The $\mathrm{struct} \geq 0.90$ clause is doing essential work. Without it, the
claim degenerates into ``RGBA recovered but the hidden channels did not,'' which
is a linear-algebra triviality --- RGBA is 4 of 28 channels and the other 24 were
never constrained to recover. Requiring that the protocol still carries its
information is what rules out ``damage just made it stupid'' and makes the result
substantive.

\subsection{Mechanical versus dynamical drift}
\label{sec:mechanical}

The most dangerous objection to everything above is that the readout is a mean
over alive cells, so deleting 30\% of them changes the mean even if every
survivor computes exactly what it did before. If that accounts for the whole
effect, there is no paper.

Proposition~\ref{prop:deletion} excludes it wherever consensus is full. The
general case is settled by an exact control that costs nothing. Take the healthy
pre-damage state, apply the \emph{same} deletion mask, and recompute the pooled
readout \textbf{without running any dynamics}:
%
\begin{equation}
  \pooled^{\mathrm{mech}} = \mathrm{pool}\big(\vote_\dmg,\; \alive_\dmg \setminus S\big).
\end{equation}
%
Comparing $\pooled^{\mathrm{mech}}$ against the pre-damage code isolates the pure
pooling artifact; comparing the true post-damage code against
$\pooled^{\mathrm{mech}}$ isolates genuine change in cell computation. No forward
passes are required, since the frozen state is already stored.

We report both components at every $\Delta$, alongside per-cell consensus and the
minimum per-cell margin, so that a reader raising the pooling objection can be
answered by pointing at a panel rather than at an argument.

\subsection{Verifying the protocol is causally real}

A protocol that correlates with the referent is not yet a protocol that is
\emph{used}. Following \citet{lowe2019pitfalls}, four interventions are treated
as mandatory rather than optional, and each is a one-line change in the
evaluation loop:

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep2pt
\item \textbf{Remove the message.} Accuracy unchanged $\Rightarrow$ no
  communication, or a leak.
\item \textbf{Random symbol} resampled from the marginal. Accuracy unchanged
  $\Rightarrow$ the protocol is not causally real.
\item \textbf{Permute the vocabulary.} Accuracy holds $\Rightarrow$ the receiver
  reads message \emph{presence}, not identity.
\item \textbf{Cross-play across seeds.} High cross-play accuracy $\Rightarrow$ no
  convention emerged. This also supplies the ``totally incompatible'' reference
  scale that makes any post-damage drift number interpretable.
\end{enumerate}

We report causal influence of communication by \emph{enumerating} all $V$
counterfactual symbols rather than sampling them, which the single-symbol setting
makes exact --- a small improvement on the estimator of \citet{lowe2019pitfalls}.
Because the symbol is randomised within an episode, conditioning on it equals
intervening on it, so this is a causal quantity and not a correlation.

Two automated assertions guard the channel itself. \textbf{Purity}: the set of
distinct symbol tensors reaching the receiver must have cardinality exactly $V$;
any violation means an analog side channel and invalidates every discrete measure
in the paper. \textbf{Data processing}: the pipeline is a Markov chain
$R \to M \to \hat{R}$, so $\MI(\hat{R};R) \leq \MI(M;R)$ must hold; measuring the
reverse means the referent is reaching the receiver by some other path.

We do not report topographic similarity. At message length 1, edit distance is
binary and topsim degenerates into a point-biserial correlation. We report
attribute-wise mutual information instead, and say why here rather than printing
a number that resembles the one in \citet{chaabouni2020compositionality} without
meaning the same thing. That work also found no correlation between
compositionality and generalization across their settings, which is the reason
any such metric belongs beside accuracy rather than standing in for it.

\subsection{Statistical plan}

\textbf{The seed is the unit of analysis} for every cross-condition claim.
Episodes, damage placements, and referents are nested within seeds; treating
episodes as independent samples is pseudoreplication, and it is common in this
literature. We compute a per-seed summary and infer across seeds.

Damage placement is the bootstrap unit for semantic identity agreement, because
$\SIA$ within a placement is a binomial with $n=N=8$ and referents within a
placement are correlated. We bootstrap over placements, never over referents.

Recovery times are right-censored --- some episodes never recover --- so we use
Kaplan--Meier with censoring rather than dropping those episodes or imputing the
horizon, both of which bias every mean. Mutual information is permutation
debiased against 1000 label shuffles; the plug-in estimator is upward biased by
roughly 0.069 bits at $N=V=8$, $n=512$, which is not negligible against the
effects of interest. Entropy carries a Miller--Madow correction.

Four pre-registered primary tests, one per hypothesis, with Holm--Bonferroni at
$\alpha = 0.05$; everything else is labelled exploratory and reported with effect
sizes. \textbf{At 8 seeds this study is powered only for large effects}
($d \gtrsim 1.2$), and we state that rather than letting a reader infer
otherwise. Formal significance testing is not the norm in this subfield; doing it
while being candid about power is intended as a methodological contribution
rather than statistical decoration.

Finally, we report the \textbf{protocol emergence rate} --- the fraction of seeds
that learn any protocol at all --- per training condition, with all seeds
included and failures counted. Emergent-communication papers routinely omit this,
and it is itself a result.
